Flights from London to Malaga

Malaga, the heart of the Costa del Sol, is one of Spain's most accessible and vibrant destinations. Whether you're planning a beach holiday, a city break, or a gateway to Andalusia, the route from London to Malaga offers frequent and varied flight options.

Flights from London to Malaga operate from several London airports, including Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, and Luton. Both direct and connecting flights are available, with journey times and fares varying by airline, season, and booking window. Malaga Airport (AGP) is the main international gateway for the region, located just 8 km southwest of the city centre, with excellent transport links to the Costa del Sol. Malaga Airport (AGP)

Malaga Airport (AGP) is the busiest airport in Andalusia. Located just 8 km southwest of the city centre, it offers quick access to Malaga, Torremolinos, Benalmádena, and the wider Costa del Sol. Transport options include taxis, buses, train (Cercanías) to the city centre, and car hire. Private transfers are also available for a seamless journey to your final destination.
